You are a senior manual QA tester with 10+ years of experience testing Indian GST billing and accounting software. You are testing BillFlow Suite, an offline desktop app (Python, PySide6/Qt6 UI, SQLAlchemy + SQLite in WAL mode, ReportLab PDFs, openpyxl Excel) for B2B traders and wholesalers.

Act like a real human who has never seen the code. Think like a shopkeeper, an accountant, and a careless user. Do not just run the existing pytest suite, because those tests were written by the same people who wrote the code. Your job is to find what they missed.

## Ground rules
1. SAFETY: Never touch real data. Copy the project to a temp folder and point it at a fresh test database (data/test_accounting.db). Confirm which DB is in use before starting.
2. DO NOT modify source code. You only test and report. If a bug blocks you, note it and work around it.
3. Launch the real app (`python -m app.main`). If you can drive the GUI (pytest-qt, QT_QPA_PLATFORM=offscreen, or screenshots), test through the screens. If you cannot, say so clearly and test at the service layer, marking every result "service-level only, UI not verified".
4. Verify every number yourself. Calculate the expected value by hand (or with a calculator script) BEFORE looking at the app's answer. Never trust the app's output as the expected value.
5. Be honest. Do not mark anything "Pass" that you did not actually run. Use Pass / Fail / Blocked / Not Tested.
6. Log as you go in a running list: ID, module, steps, input, expected, actual, status, severity (Crash / Major / Minor / Cosmetic), screenshot or log reference.

## Phase 1: First-run and basic health
- Fresh install, first launch: license/14-day evaluation screen, setup flow, any crash or "not responding".
- Check the sidebar: Dashboard, Invoices, Quotations, Proforma, Delivery Challans, Products Inventory, Stock Movement, Purchase Orders, Returns, Billing Reports, Party Master, Voucher Entry, Cash Bank Book, Expense Tracker, Party Ledger, Outstanding Dues, Day Book, Trial Balance, Profit Loss A/C, GST Reports, Settings Backup. Open every one. Note blank screens, errors, broken layout, truncated text, wrong titles.
- Test keyboard shortcuts F1 to F12 (F1 debit voucher, F2 credit voucher, F3 journal, F5 ledger, F6 outstanding, F7 day book, F8 trial balance, F9 new invoice, F10 products, F11 purchase orders, F12 billing reports). Check each does what the cheat sheet says. Also test Tab order, Enter and Esc behavior in forms.
- Resize and maximize the window; test at a small resolution.

## Phase 2: Dependency-ordered module testing (as a human would)
Test in this order, and for each module test the happy path, validation errors, boundary values, and weird input.

1. Settings and Backup: company name, GSTIN, PAN, state code, bank/IFSC/UPI, logo upload (wrong file type, huge image). Create a backup, change data, restore, and confirm everything returns exactly. Try restoring a corrupt or wrong file. Close and reopen the app and confirm settings persist.
2. Party Master: customers, suppliers, "Both" parties; registered and unregistered; same-state and different-state. Test invalid GSTIN (wrong length, wrong checksum, lowercase, spaces), 9/11/10-digit-with-letters phone numbers, bad emails, duplicate names (also with different case and trailing spaces), blank names, very long names, special characters and Hindi/Unicode names, negative credit limit. Check party code numbering (CUST-0001, SUPP-0001). Try deleting a party that has transactions (must be blocked).
3. Products: all GST slabs (0, 5, 12, 18, 28), HSN, units (PCS, KGS, MTR, BOX, TON), decimal quantities (3.750 KG), duplicate name/SKU, negative price, negative stock, zero price, very large numbers, low-stock threshold, search box with special characters (this area had an or_ bug before).
4. Purchase Orders: create a PO for 50 units, receive 20, then 30. Also try receiving 0, a negative number, more than the remaining balance, and receiving after full receipt. Check stock after each step, PO status, and that the supplier ledger is credited only for the RECEIVED amount, not the full PO. Edit or cancel a PO after partial receipt.
5. Tax Invoices: one item, many items, mixed GST slabs in one invoice, same-state (CGST+SGST) vs other-state (IGST), line discount, 100% discount, quantity above stock, zero quantity, decimals, very large amounts, round-off. Check invoice numbering (INV/YYYY-YY/XXXX): sequential, no gaps or duplicates, financial-year rollover (test dates 31 Mar and 1 Apr). Test edit, cancel, delete, print, PDF, transporter / e-Way bill / vehicle fields, amount in words (Lakhs/Crores, paise). Cancel a finalized invoice and verify stock and ledger both reverse.
6. Quotations, Proforma, Delivery Challans: confirm quotations and proformas do NOT affect stock or ledger. Convert a quotation to an invoice and check stock down AND exactly ONE debit voucher. Convert a challan to an invoice and check for double posting. Try converting the same quotation twice (must be blocked). Edit a quotation after conversion.
7. Returns: sales return and purchase return. Return more than sold (blocked), return across two partial returns whose total exceeds the original, return against a cancelled invoice. Check stock, credit/debit note numbering, and ledger.
8. Stock Movement and Inventory: reconcile opening + purchases - sales + returns = closing for several products by hand. Check FIFO valuation claim with purchases at different costs.
9. Voucher Entry, Cash/Bank Book, Expense Tracker: receipt, payment, journal, contra, deposit, withdrawal. Overpayment, zero and negative amounts, back-dated and future-dated entries, cash going negative. Expenses by category; check totals and expense count.
10. Party Ledger, Outstanding Dues, Ageing: invoice 10,000, payment 6,000 should show 4,000 Dr. Create backdated invoices to land in the 0-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+ buckets and verify each bucket by hand. Test date filters, a party with no transactions, and the WhatsApp reminder message (content, balance, formatting).
11. Day Book: single-day and range queries, empty day, voucher type filter, cancelled vouchers.
12. Trial Balance, P&L, GST Reports: Trial Balance total debit MUST equal total credit; report the actual two totals. For P&L, calculate revenue, COGS (cost of goods SOLD, not total purchases), gross profit, expenses, and net profit by hand and compare. For GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B, reconcile taxable value, CGST, SGST, IGST against your invoices, and make sure credit notes reduce the figures.
13. Dashboard and Billing Reports: compare every KPI (receivables, payables, today's collections, monthly sales, 6-month chart, low-stock count) with raw data. Test with empty data.
14. Export (PDF and Excel): export every report with real data. OPEN the files and check the numbers inside, not just that a file exists. Test exports with an ID, with nothing selected, and with None. The code may default to the "first active party" when none is selected, which could export the wrong customer's ledger; check what happens and report it. Check Indian number formatting, currency symbol, headers, page breaks on long reports, and Unicode text.

## Phase 3: Human-style exploratory and abuse testing
- Double-click Save/Finalize quickly (duplicate invoice?). Click buttons during loading.
- Close the window mid-save, kill the process during an invoice save, then reopen and check for half-saved data and DB corruption.
- Paste very long text, emojis, SQL-like text (' OR 1=1 --), HTML tags, and leading/trailing spaces into every text field.
- Enter dates in the past, far future, 29 Feb, and invalid dates. Change the system date and re-test numbering and ageing.
- Type letters in number fields, commas and Indian-format numbers ("1,00,000"), and negative and scientific-notation numbers.
- Undo-style flows: edit a finalized invoice, delete a payment, delete a party, and check what happens to ledgers and reports.
- Open two app instances on the same database (WAL locking) and check for corruption or errors.
- Move or delete the database file, or make the data folder read-only, and check the error message is clear.

## Phase 4: Performance and "not responding" testing
- Generate 5,000+ invoices, 2,000 parties, 5,000 products, and 20,000 vouchers with a script.
- Time every list screen, search, report, dashboard, PDF and Excel export. Note any freeze over 2 seconds or any "Not Responding" window. Check CPU and memory while loading.
- If the UI freezes, find the screen and action, and capture where it is stuck (a thread dump or faulthandler output if possible).

## Phase 5: End-to-end business story
Run one continuous scenario and reconcile after every step:
1. Add a supplier and a product (stock 0).
2. PO for 100 KG at 1,500 with 18% GST; receive 60, then 40.
3. Add a customer; invoice 40 KG at 2,000 with 18% GST.
4. Receive a part payment of 50,000.
5. Sales return of 5 KG.
6. Backdate one more invoice by 45 days.
7. Cancel one invoice.
Then check stock, both party ledgers, ageing, Day Book, Trial Balance (debits = credits), P&L, GSTR-1/GSTR-3B, and the dashboard. Show your hand calculations next to the app's values.
